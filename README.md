# REDEMPTIS TIARIEA - PROTOTYPE STAGE

**A game that depicts the story of the peoples of the Tiariean race, we will be playing as a ship captain commander under the command of the high commander Krist'ven - Who is serving the Yutiarinalian Realm of the Tiariean peoples...**

That will be the future vision, but for now this Phaser2D project will be hosted on the web to at the very least depict the openings of the story... As well to showcase the early concept of the overall gameplay and how the series depiction will go in the future.

# Factions
### NATIVE BORNS

## Yutiarinalian Realms
The last standing feudal bastion realm of the Tiariean race, it is known that some other feudal realms existed before was eventually merged under the Yutiarinalian's rule, the faction is led by the Flas'hanya's house amongst them all.

## Aistiaria Federation
Also known as Aistiaria Federal Republic, they were group of states that was once used to be feudal states, but their peoples broke away and form their own ruling republics, overthrowing their kings and queens (some of which were deserved, others are overthrown out of malice).

## Verdania Principality
Neutral realm, serve as financial, technological, and cultural center of the Tiariean world... It refuses to be a part of either Yutiarin nor Aistiaria, both of them depends on Verdania for economical and cultural support.

### ARTIFICIAL AIs

## Yutiarin Origins
### Harmonica
The first AI created by the Yutiarinalian's science divisions, once helped the Yutiarin to quell aways its rival the Imperica whom was once commited to violent rebellion... Ultimately Harmonica have its own agenda and turned against the Yutiarinalian while launch itself to rivalry with the Imperica.
### Imperica
Another AI division originally meant to bolster the Yutiarinalian tactical military might to measure themselves against Aistiaria, however it unfortunately misaligned and turn against the Yutiarin as a whole, trying to consolidate powers by its own.
### Evangelista
Adaptive division meant to integrates the living Tiariean to its synthetic artificial systems instead of being fully autonomous, it avoided the mistakes of the past two predeccessors.

## Aistiaria Origins
### Divinica
Autonomous units with god-like capabilities just like some of the Tiariean-folklore on their once great gods that was once around from a very long time ago, before such gods was purged and punished for their abuses of powers. The Aistiarian once exert their dominance through Divinica, however it ends up trying to wrestle with their ruling seats instead.
### Amethysca
AI entities meant to help research unknown quantum physics once buried in the ruins of the ancient Tiariean's ancestors from a long distant past... However as their research goes, they end up being misaligned and turned on their researchers companions.
### Yinyalista
This was when the Aistiarian authorities decided to do the same thing their Yutiarinalian counterpart was doing, intergrates themselves with their once dominant artificial technology, just to try and adapt to the situations they inflicted upon themselves with their blind ambitions...

## Isindor Origins
### Caliphista
Subversive agents that infiltrated the Tiariean society long before they even made their very first AI, they were the agents of the Caliphathic Eden, poised to manipulates great ancient malevolent events within the Tiariean society driving wars and strifes to the favours of Isindor, Anatra and the Caliphathic Eden.
### Doraniaca
Stolen AI repurposed by the Caliphista, to at the very least extract some values out of the Tiariean's inventions as they override them with their own philosophies and brainwash them to their wills...
### Isilica
A more advanced models but serve a different purposes for Anatra and her faction within the Caliphathic Eden...

## Ancestor Ijyiakaryai's Ruins
### The First Protectorate Guardians
Slumbering in the ancient ruins buried deep in the Ijyiakaryai's past ancestors that once opposed the ancient Tiarieans who appoint themselves as "gods", they might not be able to identify the intentions of any intruders arrives at the place they meant to defends and protect the hidden secrets...
### The Sentinels of Protected Records
These are ancient autonomous beings that might be hostile at first to any explorers until it verified they do not have any perceived malevolent causes for arriving at their holds, especially if they recognizes a familiar face to stand down and help them uncover any hidden truths...
### Forgotten Vanguards of the Old Valleys
A very long time ago the Ijyiakaryai managed to created a very sosphisticated and advanced artificial warriors, but with a very sosphisticated guardrails and grand moral doings, these beings never went to betrayal, and the Ijyiakaryai vowed to let them sleep until the very days they truly need to awake them to serve their realms again.

### INVASION FORCES
## Caliphathic Eden
A group of fanatic obliterators once serves the wills of Eden, they swept on worlds that do not aligns with the views of their total paradise, and obliterates primitives that went against their visions... Isindor once led them as something moral and different a very long time ago, until Anatra wrestle control of the whole faith and turn them to what they are today...
## Astral Union
Another invasion forces not have anything to do with the Caliphathic Eden's faith, but they are aligned to do their things against the Tiariean, the Astral Union's goals is to eradicates any traces of human chosen ones, and that they aim to obliterates any races even resembling a single similarity to both the Humans and the Katuri.
