# REDEMPTIS TIARIEA - PROTOTYPE STAGE

**A game that depicts the story of the peoples of the Tiariean race. We will be playing as a ship captain commander under the command of the High Commander Krist'ven — who is serving the Yutiarinalian Realm of the Tiariean peoples...**

That will be the future vision, but for now this Phaser 2D project will be hosted on the web to at the very least depict the openings of the story... as well as to showcase the early concept of the overall gameplay and how the series depiction will go in the future.

Further storyline after this will most likely be a downloadable Steam game in different releases.

The current build of the game is created with assistance from Claude Code, Github Copilot, Qwen Coder and OpenAI's code reviewer. With the sprite assets mostly made by my own manual hand drawing then converted to .svg sprite assets using a website called Vectron, or I create them directly from my Inkscape vector drawing app.

# Factions
### + NATIVE BORNS +

## Yutiarinalian Realms
The last standing feudal bastion realm of the Tiariean race. It is known that some other feudal realms existed before and were eventually merged under the Yutiarinalian's rule. The faction is led by the Flas'hanya's house amongst them all.

## Aistiaria Federation
Also known as the Aistiaria Federal Republic, they were a group of states that were once feudal states, but their peoples broke away and formed their own ruling republics, overthrowing their kings and queens (some of which were deserved, others overthrown out of malice).

## Verdania Principality
A neutral realm that serves as the financial, technological, and cultural center of the Tiariean world... It refuses to be a part of either Yutiarin or Aistiaria; both of them depend on Verdania for economic and cultural support.

### + ARTIFICIAL AI + 

## Yutiarin Origins
### Harmonica
The first AI created by the Yutiarinalian's science divisions. It once helped the Yutiarin quell its rival, the Imperica, who had once committed to violent rebellion... Ultimately Harmonica developed its own agenda and turned against the Yutiarinalian while launching itself into rivalry with the Imperica.
### Imperica
Another AI division originally meant to bolster the Yutiarinalian tactical military might to measure themselves against Aistiaria. However, it unfortunately misaligned and turned against the Yutiarin as a whole, trying to consolidate power on its own.
### Evangelista
An adaptive division meant to integrate the living Tiariean into its synthetic artificial systems instead of being fully autonomous. It avoided the mistakes of the past two predecessors.

## Aistiaria Origins
### Divinica
Autonomous units with god-like capabilities, just like some of the Tiariean folklore about their once-great gods that existed a very long time ago, before such gods were purged and punished for their abuses of power. The Aistiarians once exerted their dominance through Divinica; however, it ended up trying to wrestle control of their ruling seats instead.
### Amethysca
AI entities meant to help research unknown quantum physics once buried in the ruins of the ancient Tiariean's ancestors from a long distant past... However, as their research progressed, they ended up being misaligned and turned on their researcher companions.
### Yinyalista
This was when the Aistiarian authorities decided to do the same thing their Yutiarinalian counterparts were doing: integrate themselves with their once-dominant artificial technology, just to try and adapt to the situations they had inflicted upon themselves with their blind ambitions...

## Isindor Origins
### Caliphista
Subversive agents that infiltrated Tiariean society long before they even made their very first AI. They were the agents of the Caliphathic Eden, poised to manipulate great ancient malevolent events within Tiariean society, driving wars and strife to the favour of Isindor, Anatra, and the Caliphathic Eden.
### Doraniaca
Stolen AI repurposed by the Caliphista to at the very least extract some value out of the Tiariean's inventions as they overrode them with their own philosophies and brainwashed them to their wills...
### Isilica
More advanced models that serve a different purpose for Anatra and her faction within the Caliphathic Eden... Some of them are either left-wing or right-wing of Anatra herself...

## Ancestral Ijyiakaryai Origins
### Sulmakaraik
#### The First Protectorate Guardians
Slumbering in the ancient ruins buried deep in the Ijyiakaryai's past ancestors that once opposed the ancient Tiarieans who appointed themselves as "gods". They might not be able to identify the intentions of any intruders who arrive at the place they are meant to defend and protect the hidden secrets...
### Kulsirakyil
#### The Sentinels of Protected Records
These are ancient autonomous beings that might be hostile at first to any explorers until they verify that they do not have any perceived malevolent causes for arriving at their holds, especially if they recognize a familiar face to stand down and help them uncover any hidden truths...
### Ulthyriakyiai
#### Forgotten Vanguards of the Old Valleys
A very long time ago the Ijyiakaryai managed to create very sophisticated and advanced artificial warriors, but with very sophisticated guardrails and grand moral doings. These beings never went to betrayal, and the Ijyiakaryai vowed to let them sleep until the very day they truly need to awaken them to serve their realms again.

<hr>

### + INVASION FORCES + 
## Caliphathic Eden
A group of fanatic obliterators who once served the wills of Eden. They swept on worlds that do not align with the views of their total paradise and obliterated primitives that went against their visions... Isindor once led them as something moral and different a very long time ago, until Anatra wrestled control of the whole faith and turned them into what they are today...
## Astral Union
Another invasion force that has nothing to do with the Caliphathic Eden's faith, but they are aligned to do their things against the Tiariean. The Astral Union's goal is to eradicate any traces of human chosen ones, and they aim to obliterate any races even resembling a single similarity to both the Humans and the Katuri.

<hr>

### + POTENTIAL ALLIES +
## Jurisdictate Council
Opposed the Caliphathic Eden long before they interacted with the modern-day Tiarieans... They are the opposers who once served the same faith as Isindor, until the very day they rebelled and formed the Jurisdiction.
## United Galactic Federation
Main rival of the Astral Union. They seek to stop them from destroying more alien races and enslaving the galaxies... They once formed in response against Grimarous Prime and his bioforms, then they opposed the Astral Union.

### + REDEMPTION PARTNER +
## Elven Ijyiakaryai
MERASSYILRI<br>
NIARIEKYIAI - KISARAKYIL - SAKIGARAIK - HAYAKIYIAI<br><br>
Even though the Ijyiakaryai and the Tiariean ancestors once had a turbulent past, they both fell victim to the same Isindor. For the Ijyiakaryai, the Isindor was once an ally against the bad ancestral Tiariean. They eventually ended up betraying the Ijyiakaryai, and ever since then the elves have hated the Isindor and what their Caliphathic Eden currently represents under the manipulation of Anatra.

## Free Innomini
Runaway Refugees<br><br>
A stray-away state of peoples who escaped the Isindor and now seek refuge with either the Tiarieans or the Ijyiakaryai, or any other races that still do not despise their racial origin. They aim to save their peoples from Isindor — only those who they can save.
